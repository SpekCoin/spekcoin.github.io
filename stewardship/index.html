Stewardship

The allocation below was generated from the node source.

The genesis block mints 30,000,000 coins once, and every block after it pays only the ordinary reward. The allocation is two parts, each in vaults whose spending rules are fixed in consensus.

The stewardship fund: 7,500,000 coins

  • A launch slice of 1,500,000 coins, spendable once coinbase maturity has passed, so grants and audits can start at launch, and ten tranches that unlock over time (table below).
  • One council of 5 keys spends with any 3. There are no reserve keys and no recovery path.
  • Every spend is staged first and waits 86,400 blocks (about 30 days) in public view; 3 of 5 guardians can cancel it back to the vault in that time.

The founders: 22,500,000 coins

  • The founders, each with their own vault spent by 2 of 3 of that founder's keys.
  • Each founder's share unlocks in five yearly tranches.
  • Staged founder spends wait the same 86,400 blocks, and any 1 of the founder's 3 guardians can cancel one.

What is not on this page yet

Council members and their key fingerprints, the payout policy and the payout ledger will be published soon. Every spend from these outputs will be listed by transaction id and reviewable.
