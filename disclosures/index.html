Disclosures

Two facts qualify every claim on this site. They appear on the home page and on every cryptography and security page.

SQIsign is not a NIST standard

SQIsign is a Round 3 candidate in NIST's additional-signature process (advanced in May 2026, with about two years of evaluation ahead). "NIST security level 1" names a security category, not an approval.

Reviewed only by AI models

The founders used Claude AI models to build and review Spek, and the code has been reviewed only by AI models (Claude). No independent human audit, cryptographer review, or SQIsign-author review took place before launch. Anthropic, the maker of Claude, is not a partner in this project and does not endorse it.
